# CLAUDE.md — Bharat Truck Simulator (formerly ChairDrive)

**https://bharattrucksimulator.com** — **Bharat Truck Simulator** (renamed from ChairDrive 2026-10-03, Gyan's ask; the repo,
Worker name, `chairdrive.*` storage keys and other internal ids keep the old name on purpose) — is a free browser game: drive a truck down an endless Indian highway
from a first-person cab. Steer with the keyboard, touch, your phone as a tethered wheel, or
**chair mode** (phone strapped to an office chair laid on its back; turn the chair to steer).
Built by Gyan Pratap Singh (owner, decides everything; writes English/Hinglish).
